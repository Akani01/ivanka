Support

Frequently asked questions

Useful answers about the closet and marketplace.

What does Ivanka & Stell Closet sell?

Our supplied range includes apparel, activewear, swimwear, resort wear, yoga mats, fashion accessories and Fairy Natural Beauty +1 body, face and hair care.

Where is the business based?

The business details list Kinshasa in the DRC and Johannesburg in South Africa. Worldwide enquiries are welcome; delivery arrangements must be confirmed for your destination.

When are you open?

Tuesday to Saturday: 07:00–19:00. Sunday: 09:00–15:00. Monday: closed. Confirm the location and local time with the team before visiting.

How can I check a size, ingredient or availability?

Contact us with the item name and your question before ordering. Product information and availability vary by item and seller.

Do I need an account?

You can browse available products without signing in. An account is required to use the cart, checkout, order history, wishlist and other account tools.

How do I pay?

The supplied checkout supports card payments through Yoco and cash. Availability depends on your order, seller and payment configuration. Follow the payment instructions shown at checkout.

Where can I find my orders?

Sign in and open My orders from your profile menu or the mobile navigation. Check both the order status and the payment status.

What about returns or a problem with an order?

Contact the team with your order reference and explain the issue. Confirm the applicable return conditions before buying. Statutory rights are not replaced by the website’s processes.

Can I sell or deliver through the marketplace?

Use Seller hub to create or manage a store profile. For delivery opportunities, sign in to Courier jobs or contact the team.

Can I install the website as an app?

On a supported browser, use the Install app option when available. On iPhone or iPad, use Safari’s Share menu and Add to Home Screen.
