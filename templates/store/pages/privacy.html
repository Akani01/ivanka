Privacy policy

Information about data used to provide accounts, shopping, orders and delivery.

Scope & contact

This notice covers information used through {{ brand.name }} and its marketplace features. For privacy questions, access requests or corrections, contact {{ brand.email }} or use our contact page.

Information used by the service

  • Account and profile details you provide when registering, signing in or using account features.
  • Recipient details, phone number, delivery address and, when provided for delivery, location coordinates.
  • Cart contents, wishlist selections, orders, product choices, payment status and transaction references.
  • Seller details, courier information, order messages, reviews, return requests and support enquiries.
  • Essential session, security and app-storage information used to operate the site.

Why information is used

Information is used to manage accounts, fulfil and communicate about orders, arrange deliveries, process payment status, provide seller or courier tools, respond to enquiries and maintain service security and transaction records.

Payments & delivery providers

Card checkout takes place through Yoco. Enter card details only in the payment provider’s checkout. Relevant order and delivery details may be shared with the seller, payment provider or delivery participants as needed to complete the service.

Location & optional features

Location-dependent services may require browser permission or coordinates you supply. You can manage browser permissions on your device. App installation and optional notification features can also be managed through your browser or device settings.

Storage, access & requests

Contact the team to request access to, correction of or deletion of your information. Identity verification may be needed. Some transaction, accounting or dispute records may need to be retained even after an account request. Ask the team for the retention details applicable to your records.

Cookies & third-party services

See our cookie information for essential sessions and app storage. The interface also loads fonts and icons from external providers. Third-party sign-in and payment services handle information under their own notices.

Updates

This page may be updated when the service or its information-handling arrangements change. Contact the team if you need clarification about a particular use of your information.
