Shopping & checkout

  1. Browse the shop or search for a product.
  2. Choose an available option and quantity.
  3. Sign in, add it to your cart and review your items.
  4. Add a complete delivery address, including phone, city, province or region and postal code.
  5. Review the order and the payment option before proceeding.

Orders & payments

Use My orders to check the status of purchases. An order being created does not mean its payment is complete; check the payment status separately.

For a payment issue, note the order reference and contact us. Never send your card number, security code, password or one-time code to support.

Delivery, returns & product questions

For delivery updates, use your order reference when contacting the team. Confirm delivery options, charges, return conditions and any product restrictions before purchasing.

For an incorrect or damaged item, contact us with the order reference and an explanation of the problem.
