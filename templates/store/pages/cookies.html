Cookies & app storage

How essential website functions use your browser.

Essential cookies

The website uses session cookies to maintain sign-in and security cookies to protect requests, such as changes to your cart, profile and orders. Blocking these cookies can prevent those features from working.

Installed app & offline page

The service worker stores a general offline page so that a connection problem can be explained. This package does not cache authenticated pages, API responses, orders, checkout data or payment screens.

External resources

Fonts and icon styles are loaded from the external providers used by the interface. Other integrations added by the site operator may have their own notices.

Your controls

You can clear site data and manage cookies, permissions and installed apps in your browser settings. Clearing site data can sign you out. For questions, contact the team.
