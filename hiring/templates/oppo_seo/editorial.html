About these education guides

Practical explanations for learners and families using OppoGlobe.

What these pages cover

These guides help you organise applications, research institutions, compare costs and practise for exams. They contain original explanatory text and illustrative planning examples. They do not represent a university, a bursary provider, NSFAS or a government department. They cannot decide your eligibility or guarantee a place, funding or an examination result.

Sources and dates

Official source links appear on each guide. Requirements, funding rules, programme details and closing dates can change. Follow the provider’s current instructions if they differ from a guide. The publication date identifies when this version of the text was prepared; it is not a claim that every external page is reviewed daily.

Corrections and questions

If you find an error, use OppoGlobe’s contact page. Include the guide’s web address, the wording you believe is incorrect and a link to the relevant official information. Do not include identity documents, passwords or private student records. For an application decision or account-specific request, contact the institution or provider directly.

Listings and advertising

Community listings and sponsored recommendations are separate from these guides. A listing or advertisement is not evidence of accreditation or an endorsement of its claims. Verify an organisation through official channels before paying or sharing sensitive information.
