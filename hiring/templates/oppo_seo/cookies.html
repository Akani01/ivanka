Cookie preferences

Choose whether this template may allow optional services.

What the preference control stores

The cookie control stores a small record named og_optional_services_v1 in your browser’s local storage for up to 180 days. It records your choice and the time you made it. It does not contain your name or application documents. If local storage is unavailable, your choice applies only while this page is open and the question may appear again.

Essential features and outside services

Signing in, security, payment checkout and other requested features may use storage or connect to other services. The optional-services preference does not turn off those essential features. Existing photographs and fonts may also be requested from external hosts. Read the site privacy policy for the operator’s wider data practices and contact details.

Optional services

The supplied guide pages do not load optional analytics or Google advertising scripts. Allowing optional services records your preference; it does not itself activate an advertising service. If advertising or analytics is enabled later, its implementation must respect your choice and any applicable consent requirements. Google explains its partner-site data use in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Change your choice

You can reopen the preference control from the footer. Clearing browser storage removes the saved record. Preferences are specific to this browser; changing them here does not change choices on another device or another website.
