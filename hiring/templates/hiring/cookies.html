01 / The essentials

What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information a website can store in your browser. Related technologies, such as local storage, may also retain information on your device. Some help a website remember that you are signed in; others may support preferences, measurement or third-party services.

OppoGlobe brings together properties, education, jobs, community content, car wash and other services. Different features may need different browser technologies to operate.

02 / How they work

Types of cookies and storage

Necessary

Essential functionality

Session and security-related cookies may be needed for sign-in, account protection and safe form submission. Turning these off in your browser may stop important features from working.

Optional, where enabled

Preferences

Preference storage may remember choices such as display settings or cookie selections so you do not have to repeat them.

Optional, where enabled

Analytics and performance

Measurement tools may help site operators understand visits and improve reliability. Whether they are used, and which providers are involved, depends on the site's actual configuration.

Optional, where enabled

Advertising and embedded services

Advertising networks, video embeds, maps or payment services may use their own cookies or browser storage when those integrations are loaded. Their privacy practices can differ from OppoGlobe's.

03 / Your controls

Manage your cookie preferences

Use these controls to record your preferences on this device. Necessary cookies remain enabled because they support core functionality.

Necessary cookies

Sign-in, security and basic operation

Important: These controls record a preference in your browser. They do not, by themselves, prevent third-party scripts from setting cookies. Your Django backend and any analytics or advertising scripts must check consent before loading optional services.

Browser controls

You can also clear or block cookies in your browser settings. Doing so may sign you out or reset preferences.

04 / Connected experiences

Third-party services

Some OppoGlobe experiences may involve external providers, such as maps, embedded media, payment processing or advertising. When you use these features, the provider may process information under its own policies. Do not assume all such services are active on every page.

For a full production policy, the specific cookies, provider names, purposes and retention periods should be verified against the services actually deployed on OppoGlobe.

05 / Stay informed

Updates and questions

Cookie practices can change when features or providers change. This policy should be updated to reflect those changes, with a verified last-updated date.

For questions, visit our Contact Us page. You can also explore the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
