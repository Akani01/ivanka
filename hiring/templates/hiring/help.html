Popular help guides

Select a topic or search to narrow these guides.

How do I create an OppoGlobe account?

Open the account or sign-up option from OppoGlobe, choose the appropriate account type and follow the registration steps. Where available, personal accounts can use Google sign-in. Keep your login details private.

Where can I explore everything on OppoGlobe?

Use the top navigation, desktop sidebar or mobile bottom navigation. These take you to the same platform areas, including properties, community, reels, jobs, education and services. Explore OppoGlobe.

How do I list a property?

Sign in and choose the property listing option. Enter accurate property details, location, price and images. Check the listing information before submitting. Go to properties.

How do I request a property booking?

Open a property that offers bookings, review its availability and conditions, select the relevant dates and follow the booking steps. Payment options depend on the listing and checkout configuration. Confirm the details before paying.

What if I cannot sign in?

Check the email or username and password you used, and try the password reset option if available. If the issue continues, contact support without sending your password or verification codes.

How can I protect my account?

Use a unique password, protect your email account and avoid sharing one-time codes. Check links before signing in and report suspicious requests through the contact page.

Which payment methods can I use?

Where enabled, OppoGlobe booking or service flows may offer Yoco-hosted card checkout and other displayed options. Only use payment methods shown in the relevant booking flow, and keep a record of the transaction.

What should I do about a payment problem?

Keep the booking reference, amount and transaction time. Check whether a payment confirmation was issued, then contact support with those details. Do not include your full card number, PIN or password.

How can I browse jobs and education opportunities?

Open the Jobs or Education section from the navigation. Review eligibility, deadlines and the application instructions on each opportunity.

How do I avoid suspicious opportunities?

Check the organisation and its official contact information before sharing documents. Be cautious about upfront fees, unrealistic promises or requests for passwords and banking credentials.

Where do I find maintenance services?

Open the Maintenance area. Review available service information and communicate the problem clearly before confirming any arrangement.

How do I access car wash services?

Visit Car Wash, allow location access if you choose, and review nearby providers, available services and prices before requesting a wash.
