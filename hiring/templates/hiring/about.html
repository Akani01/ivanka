Why we exist

Opportunity should feel closer to home.

Finding a place to live, applying for a job or bursary, and arranging practical help often means switching between different services. OppoGlobe brings these discoveries into one place, with a focus on the communities and people behind them.

Our mission is to make everyday opportunities easier to discover and navigate, while helping people and businesses connect through useful listings, community experiences and direct communication.

We want people to understand what is being offered, who they are dealing with and what steps to take next. That means readable information, practical tools and clear expectations—not just attractive listings.

OppoGlobe is a platform that helps people discover and connect. Availability, eligibility, service quality and transaction terms depend on the individual listing or provider.

A practical guide

From discovery to a decision.

For people exploring

  • Choose the area of OppoGlobe that matches your goal, whether property, education, work or a service.
  • Read the full listing, including eligibility, location, price, timing and any conditions.
  • Use the available contact or messaging tools to clarify information before committing.
  • Keep records of important conversations, booking terms and payment confirmations.

For businesses and contributors

  • Describe what you offer accurately, including service areas, prices and availability.
  • Use clear, relevant images and up-to-date descriptions so people know what to expect.
  • Respond to enquiries respectfully and provide the information needed to make informed choices.
  • Do not post misleading claims, copied material without permission or requests for unnecessary sensitive information.
Trust & transparency

Know what you're agreeing to.

A useful platform also helps people recognise uncertainty. Listing details and community posts may come from third parties, and being visible on OppoGlobe does not automatically guarantee a provider, employer or property is verified.

Before you pay or share details

  • Confirm the identity of the person or organisation you are dealing with.
  • For properties, verify the location, ownership or authority to list, and written booking terms.
  • For jobs and bursaries, check official requirements and be cautious of suspicious upfront fees.
  • For services, agree on the final price, timing and what happens if the service is cancelled.

Privacy and responsible use

Share only the personal information needed for the relevant interaction. Avoid posting identity numbers, banking credentials or private contact details in public comments.

Review OppoGlobe's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for the platform's published rules. Contact support if you encounter suspicious or misleading activity.

Payment information

Clear checkout information matters.

For eligible transactions where card checkout is enabled, OppoGlobe can direct customers to a Yoco-powered payment flow. Card information should be entered through the payment provider's secure checkout, not sent to a seller in messages. Cash may be available for certain services, depending on the transaction.

Always check the amount, payment method, cancellation terms and confirmation before paying. Payment options can differ between services and listings.

Frequently asked

Good questions deserve clear answers.

A quick guide to how the platform works and what to check before taking action.

What is OppoGlobe?

OppoGlobe is a South African digital platform bringing together property discovery, job opportunities, education information, community content and selected local services.

Does OppoGlobe own the properties shown?

Listings may be created by third-party owners or representatives. A property appearing on the platform does not itself establish ownership or guarantee availability. Confirm the details directly before making commitments.

Are jobs and bursaries guaranteed?

No. OppoGlobe helps users discover opportunities. Employers, institutions and programme providers determine eligibility, application decisions and closing dates. Verify requirements with the official organisation.

How do payments work?

Payment options depend on the service and checkout configuration. Where enabled, eligible card payments may use Yoco-powered checkout; some services may also offer cash. Check the payment instructions shown for your transaction.

How can I report suspicious content?

Use available reporting features where offered, or contact OppoGlobe through its contact page. Include the listing or post details, but avoid sharing passwords or sensitive banking information.

How can a business participate?

Businesses can explore the relevant account and listing options on the platform. Accurate descriptions, pricing, availability and responsive communication help users make informed choices.
